Option D — Consistency Grid
Every PAX, every day, one screen · July 2026

The whole month at a glance

One row per PAX, one square per day, grouped by team and sorted by score inside each team. It is not a ranking — it is a pattern. Solid green rows and gaps are both obvious instantly, and a team's block reads as one shape.

Why it motivates: streak psychology. Nobody wants to be the one row with a hole in it, and the row you are most aware of is your own — this is the "don't break the chain" mechanic applied to the whole AO. The footer bar shows which days the community struggles with (weekends, holidays), which is a Site Q planning tool as much as a motivator.
Watch out: the most public-shaming of the five. It reads as supportive only if a Miss (reported, amber) is visibly distinct from a no-show (orange) — the reward is for reporting, not only for hitting. Consider showing it team-by-team rather than AO-wide.
