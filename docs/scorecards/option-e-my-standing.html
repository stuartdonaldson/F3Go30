Shown at phone width — this option is designed to sit at the top of the existing dashboard, above My Team.
Why it motivates: the only option framed entirely around the man looking at it. It never shows him a list he is at the bottom of — it shows him the two men either side and the smallest action that moves him. Cheapest to ship: it is a card, not a new screen.
Watch out: low ceiling on team competition. Pairs naturally with B (Team Race) — E answers "how am I doing", B answers "how are we doing".
