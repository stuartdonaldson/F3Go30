Individual leaderboard · July 2026

Top HIMs this month

The direct successor to the HIM Score sheet: one ranked list of every PAX, with the top three lifted onto a podium. The bar is the score; the colour is the team, so an individual result still says something about a team.

Why it motivates: a PAX always knows their number and the exact gap to the man directly above them — the callout under your own row names him and the exact number.
Watch out: a 36-row ladder puts the bottom third on public display every day. If that lands badly the list can be cut to "top 10, then you and your two neighbours" — the anchored row and its gap callout already work that way.
