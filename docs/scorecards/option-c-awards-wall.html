Named honours, individual and team · July 2026

July honours

Instead of one ranking with one winner, several named awards — each on a different axis, so a man who will never top the total score can still take something home. Every award names the runner-up, which is what keeps it contested.

Team trophies

Team-level honours use the same shape. Consistency and turnout are rewarded alongside raw score.

Your cabinet

The same wall from one PAX's point of view — what you hold, and the nearest thing you could still take.

Why it motivates: many ways to win. The Awards Wall rewards the behaviours the Go30 actually wants — showing up, Qing, EHing, bringing fellowship — not just the highest number.
Watch out: awards need to feel earned. With a small AO the same three men can sweep every category — Slingshot takes two here. The runner-up line on every card is what makes a permanently-locked category visible, so it can be retired or re-cut.
