Go30 Scorecards
Five competitive-display options · UI mockups

What these are

Five ways the Go30 app could present competition and reward, all drawn from the same mock July month so they can be compared directly. Today's HIM Score and Team Score sheets answer "who is top" and "which team is top" in two static tables; the app can do considerably more, because it already holds every PAX's day-by-day values, bonuses, streaks and team on the client. None of these options need a new server field.

Read the trade-offs in README.md — each mockup also carries its own "why it motivates / watch out" note at the bottom of the page.

A

HIM Ladder

Podium plus a full ranked list of every PAX, bar-coded by team, with your own row anchored and the gap to the man above spelled out.

IndividualClosest to HIM ScoreRanks everyone publicly
B

Team Race

Team averages as lanes on a race track, a day-by-day cumulative chart showing lead changes, and a promoted weekly head-to-head.

Recommended pairingTeamClosest to Team Score
C

Awards Wall

Eight named individual honours and three team trophies on different axes, each naming its runner-up, plus a personal trophy cabinet.

Many ways to winRewards behaviour, not just score
D

Consistency Grid

Every PAX, every day, one screen — a heatmap grouped by team, with an AO-wide hit-rate bar along the bottom.

Whole communitySite Q toolMost exposing
E

Where I Stand

A personal card: rank dial, the two men either side of you, you vs team vs AO, and the next milestone within reach.

Recommended pairingPersonalCheapest to ship
Recommendation: ship E (personal standing) and B (team race) together — E gives every man a reason to open the app whether he is first or last, B gives the AO something to argue about. Add C once a month of data exists to award from. A and D are the highest-risk on morale and are best held back or scoped to a single team.
