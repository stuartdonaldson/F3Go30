Option B — Team Race
Team-vs-team standings · July 2026

The race

The Team Score sheet re-cast as a race track. Each lane is a team, the puck sits at that team's average score, so a six-man team and a nine-man team are compared fairly. The vertical marker is the month’s par: 1 point per day, every day.

How the month unfolded

Cumulative team average, day by day. Lead changes are the story — a team that was third on the 10th and first on the 25th is the one everybody talks about at the Coffeeteria.

This week's head-to-head

One pairing promoted each week — a rivalry is easier to care about than a six-way table. Rotates automatically so every team gets a turn.

Why it motivates: peer pressure with a shared goal — you are not letting your six down, and a team average means a low-scoring PAX who reports still helps.
Watch out: averages punish a team for recruiting an FNG mid-month. If that bites, switch the lane to "% of possible points earned since each man joined".
