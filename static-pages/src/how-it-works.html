Go30 — How it Works

THE CORE MISSION

Go30 is a simple way to build momentum around the habits that matter most. Each day you choose one challenge, own your effort, and let your team help keep you honest.

THREE LAYERS OF CHANGE

Adapted from the Identity/Process/Outcome model in James Clear's Atomic Habits — see also his Habits Cheat Sheet.

W
Identity (Who) — Change your beliefs about who you are.
H
Processes (How) — Change your daily habits and systems.
W
Outcomes (What) — Change your specific results.

YOUR DAILY MISSION

Each day scores one of three ways: Hit (1) if you did it, Miss (0) if you didn't, or Failed to report (−1) if you forget to check in — so it always pays to report, even a miss.

  1. Choose your challenge — keep it small, specific, and easy to measure.
  2. Record your score — check in each day and let the dashboard show your streak, your score, and your team's momentum.
  3. Beat the deadline — report by 10:00 AM the next day, or the system will mark it as a miss for you.

BONUS POINTS

Beyond your daily challenge, log EHing an FNG, a Fellowship moment, taking a Q, or an Inspire moment for extra points — EHing an FNG counts most. Add these from the check-in page's Bonus Points section, or directly in the Bonus Tracker sheet. EHing FNG, Q Point, and Inspire need a link to Slack as evidence before they count.

STRENGTH IN NUMBERS

You can go it alone — but you won't go as far. Team up with 3–5 fellow HIMs who share your drive, usually grouped by AO since Shieldlock works best with proximity, and let them help you stay accountable. Joining a team is optional but highly encouraged — you can also participate solo.

Men who pursue hard things together become men who achieve them.
