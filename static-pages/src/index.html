Why your team matters

Your team is your accountability and support structure for this Go30 month — the people who notice if you go missing and cheer when you hit a milestone. Go30 works because someone else knows what you committed to.

AO-based team: pick your regular F3 AO (e.g. Iron Mtn, The Ranch) — your teammates are the other PAX who signed up from that same AO.

Custom team: not tied to one AO, or want to team up with specific PAX? Type a team name (a Slack channel, a nickname your crew agrees on) — anyone who types that same name lands on your team.

FAQ

What counts as a "Hit"?
Doing the one daily challenge you committed to at signup. Check in every day — Hit, Miss, or (if you forget) it's marked Failed to report the next morning.
What's the check-in deadline?
10:00 AM the next day. Miss that window with no check-in and the system marks it a Failed to report for you — it always pays to report, even a miss.
How do Bonus Points work?
Log EHing an FNG, a Fellowship moment, taking a Q, or an Inspire moment from the Bonus Points screen (Menu → Log Bonus Points). EHing FNG, Q Point, and Inspire each need a Slack link as evidence before they count.
Can I change my team after signing up?
Yes — update it from the signup flow at any time; teams are just a shared name (AO-based or custom), not a fixed roster.
I can't find my check-in page. What do I do?
Use Menu → Send Feedback to reach your Site Q, or open the Tracker sheet directly from the dashboard's spreadsheet icon (top right) — every PAX's data lives there as a fallback.
What does a missed check-in cost me?
A Miss scores 0; a Failed to report (no check-in at all) scores −1 — reporting a miss honestly is always better than not reporting.

THE CORE MISSION

Go30 is a simple way to build momentum around the habits that matter most. Each day you choose one challenge, own your effort, and let your team help keep you honest.

THREE LAYERS OF CHANGE

Adapted from the Identity/Process/Outcome model in James Clear's Atomic Habits — see also his Habits Cheat Sheet.

W
Identity (Who) — Change your beliefs about who you are.
H
Processes (How) — Change your daily habits and systems.
W
Outcomes (What) — Change your specific results.

YOUR DAILY MISSION

Each day scores one of three ways: Hit (1) if you did it, Miss (0) if you didn't, or Failed to report (−1) if you forget to check in — so it always pays to report, even a miss.

  1. Choose your challenge — keep it small, specific, and easy to measure.
  2. Record your score — check in each day and let the dashboard show your streak, your score, and your team's momentum.
  3. Beat the deadline — report by 10:00 AM the next day, or the system will mark it as a miss for you.

BONUS POINTS

Beyond your daily challenge, log EHing an FNG, a Fellowship moment, taking a Q, or an Inspire moment for extra points — EHing an FNG counts most. Add these from the check-in page's Bonus Points section, or directly in the Bonus Tracker sheet. EHing FNG, Q Point, and Inspire need a link to Slack as evidence before they count.

STRENGTH IN NUMBERS

You can go it alone — but you won't go as far. Team up with 3–5 fellow HIMs who share your drive, usually grouped by AO since Shieldlock works best with proximity, and let them help you stay accountable. Joining a team is optional but highly encouraged — you can also participate solo.
