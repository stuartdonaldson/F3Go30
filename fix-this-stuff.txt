CALIBRATION — why 12s, and why 8s was wrong
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do not re-derive these numbers from Axiom's durationMs. That is the SERVER-side
figure. The client round trip includes the 302 hop to
script.googleusercontent.com/macros/echo, which roughly doubles it. Calibrating
on the server number is precisely the mistake I made in v2.4.8.1.

Measured client-side round trip for a signup identify, no abort:
   2026-07-28 05:24Z (SIT loaded): min 3.3s  median 4.8s  p90 6.5s  max 9.1s
                                   + 1 of 12 lost (browser gave up at 29s)
   2026-07-28 14:15Z (SIT idle):   min 3.5s  median 4.5s  p90/max 9.5s
                                   + 0 of 10 lost

So healthy calls reach ~9.5s. 12s sits just above that. 8s did not.

Worst case for a lost read is now 12s + 12s = 24s before the banner appears.
That is the open question below.
